Learnup (런업) Privacy Policy

Effective: July 8, 2026 · App: Learnup (package com.pixelct.learnup) · Provider: PixelCT

Learnup does not collect any personal information. It is an offline learning app for children to trace and learn letters. It does not connect to the internet and does not transmit or store any personal data externally.

1. Information We Collect

Learnup collects no personal information — no name, email, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. There is no sign-up, login, or account.

2. Data Stored Only on the Device

Learning progress, rewards (stars, garden/farm), and practice words entered by a parent are stored only in the device's local storage (a local database). This data is never sent to any server and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. It does not identify any individual.

3. Network and Permissions

  • The released app has no internet permission and works fully offline.
  • It requests no sensitive permissions — no microphone, camera, location, contacts, or storage.
  • Reading words aloud uses the operating system's on-device text-to-speech; it is output-only and does not use the microphone.
  • Sound effects and fonts are bundled in the app, so nothing is downloaded at runtime.

4. Third Parties, Ads, and Analytics

There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party data sharing. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share.

5. Children's Privacy

Learnup is directed to children. Because it collects no personal information, no processing of children's personal data occurs under the U.S. COPPA, the EU GDPR-K, or applicable local laws. Parent-facing features such as payments, external links, and settings are placed behind a parental gate so children cannot access them on their own.

6. Payments

Learnup is currently free with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. If paid features are introduced later, purchases will be placed behind the parental gate and this policy will be updated.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as laws or the service change. Any revision will be posted on this page with its effective date.
